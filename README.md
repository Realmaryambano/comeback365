## 🗿 Why This Exists

Today, September 1, is Day 001. This isn't a side project I'm starting because I'm bored - it's the biggest comeback I've ever attempted, and it's starting right before the hardest stretch of my life hits, on purpose.

The goal: become a top AI/ML engineer, and be ready to take part in any competition, hackathon, or opportunity without a second thought. I'm ready to invest everything - time, health, distractions, fun - into making that real.

I'm doing this because:

- 💪 **I need to prove something to myself.** Not to anyone watching - to me.
- 🌍 **I want to completely change my life.** Not tweak it. Change it.
- 🏆 **I want to become the best version of myself** - someone who shows up for competitions, hackathons, and opportunities without hesitation.

All three are true at once. None of them are exaggerations.

## ⏱️ The Daily Standard

There's no single fixed number, because pretending university, Alimah studies, and a full-time comeback all fit into an identical time block every day would be a lie. The real rule is:

- 🕐 Some days: 1 hour
- 🕑 Other days: 2 hours
- 🕒 On the best days: 3+ hours
- ✅ **Every single day: something.** No day gets skipped just because it wasn't a "big" day.

---

## 📈 What Counts as a Win

A successful day means real, tangible output - not just intentions:

- 💻 Coding
- 📚 Learning
- 🛠️ Building something

In practice, that usually looks like one or more of:

- 🧩 A DSA problem solved and actually understood - not just copy-pasted from a solution
- 🤖 Time spent on ML/AI fundamentals: a course module, a paper, a concept, a model built or fine-tuned
- 🏗️ Progress on a real project that goes in the portfolio - not a tutorial I'll never revisit
- 🎥 Something created and shared publicly - a post, a thread, a video, a write-up of what I learned that day

Not "I thought about it." Not "I opened the laptop and closed it." Actual coding, actual learning, actual building - even if it's small.

---

## ⚠️ The Bad Day Rule

Bad days will happen. University will be heavy. Alimah studies will be draining. Life will get in the way.

The rule on a bad day is simple:

> **Do at least 10 minutes.**

Ten minutes of something real is enough to keep the chain unbroken. It's not about the ten minutes - it's about refusing to let a hard day become a zero day.

## 📂 How Progress Is Tracked

No 365-line checkbox wall in this README - that turns into noise within two weeks and stops meaning anything. Instead:

- Daily entries live in a `/logs` folder - `day-001.md`, `day-002.md`, and so on
- Each log is short: what I did, what I learned, what I struggled with, what's next
- The **commit history itself becomes the real timeline** of this year

This README is the anchor - the reason and the rules. The logs are the evidence.

### Suggested repo structure

```
comeback365/
├── README.md
├── logs/
│   ├── day-001.md
│   ├── day-002.md
│   └── ...
├── projects/
│   └── (real, portfolio-worthy builds live here)
├── dsa/
│   └── (problems solved, organized by topic or platform)
└── content/
    └── (drafts or links to posts, threads, videos)
```
